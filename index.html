01 / About

Between the business
and the build.

Good work starts with why. I sit close to the people using what I build — analysts, stakeholders, whoever is on the other side of the problem — and design around the questions they are actually trying to answer, not the ones that are convenient to model.

Most of my attention now goes to applied AI: putting LLMs where they genuinely earn their place, and being straight about where they do not. In practice that means caring about evaluation, retrieval quality, latency and cost per call — the unglamorous parts that decide whether a demo survives contact with real users. The data platform underneath is what makes any of it trustworthy, which is why I like working at both ends.

Everything goes through version control and CI/CD, so deployments stay predictable and boring. And I learn fastest by building things myself.
